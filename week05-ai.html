1. Customer Service using AI

Many companies are starting to use AI for their Customer Services. It has been predicted that AI will be able to fully resolve over 80% of issues by 2029, however so far only 20% are meeting expectations. These tools are meant to be more efficient than humans and have faster responses for customer service. The AI's are also trained to have more sympathy for their customer service, since humans wouldn't be as sympathetic.

2. My Opinion

I think using AI for companies is a bad idea, since AI might not be able to solve people's issues correctly. They could mess up on some instructions and even in some scenarios make the situation worse. But if AI can meet people's needs very well, it might work.

3. General Idea

Companies are using AI for Customer Service. They are trained to perform better than humans. I'm not sure if it's a good idea, but if it works out the only issue would be jobs that are lost, which is still big.
